Privacy Policy

PixPrompt Lab — Last updated: August 2026

PixPrompt Lab ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by Shami Academy. This page explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and the choices available to you. By using PixPrompt Lab, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Information stored only on your device:

  • Prompts you mark as favorites (saved locally on your phone, not sent to us)
  • Search text you type inside the App (used only to filter results on your device)

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect your name, email, or phone number.

Advertising

PixPrompt Lab displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and usage data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example, users in the EEA and UK), the App shows a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) letting you choose whether personalized ads are shown. You can revisit this choice anytime from the App's menu → Manage Ad Consent.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — displays banner and interstitial ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — manages ad consent choices
  • Image hosting — prompt preview images are loaded from publicly hosted links (e.g. Archive.org) and are not tied to your identity

The App also lets you optionally open Google Gemini or OpenAI ChatGPT from a prompt's detail screen. If you choose to do so, your use of those apps/websites is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Permissions

The App requests internet access only, used to load the prompt library and display ads. No camera, contacts, location, or storage permissions are requested.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Limited technical data (such as device identifiers used for ad delivery) is shared only with Google AdMob as described above.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
